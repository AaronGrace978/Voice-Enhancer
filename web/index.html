VocalLift

A singing engine you train yourself

For voices that know the tune

Land the note.
Keep the voice.

VocalLift is the part of singing that Auto-Tune flattens and a song generator invents. It hears the note you were reaching for, rebuilds the crack, and leaves the vibrato that makes it yours.

  1. 01
    Split the performance

    Pitch, tone, and breath come apart. A crack is a short break in the pitch, not a new note.

  2. 02
    Move only the intent

    The slow melody slides into the key, or onto a guide track of the song. Vibrato stays put.

  3. 03
    Stitch the voice back

    Vowels are resung. Consonants stay yours. A Colab notebook can fine-tune a resynthesizer on your tone.
